The positioning statement that decides your revenue.

Your positioning statement is the sentence that tells people why to choose you. Get it wrong and everything you spend on marketing, packaging, and your website is spent on the wrong message. Get it right and revenue moves — often within a year. The product didn't change in any of these cases. Only the reason to choose you changed.

Domino's · 1973

Domino's

Before: "Domino's is a pizza shop."

Volvo
