**Headline:** Singapore is near-saturated. Roughly **3.6–4.4M real reachable accounts** on each platform, essentially the entire adult population. This is not a discovery question ("are people there?") — it's a **what-are-they-paying-attention-to** question.

---

## 2. Where the view/engagement time actually goes

**Time spent (per month, SG users):**
- **TikTok = 34h29m/month** — the single highest-time platform in SG.
- **YouTube = 29h45m** (the only real rival).
- Average SG social time = **2h02m/day**.

**TikTok is the attention winner.** It out-consumes every other platform on raw time. For "what are people dedicating hours to," TikTok is the answer — not Instagram.

**Instagram content that wins engagement (SG):**
- **Reels generate 67% higher engagement than standard posts.**
- **Categories that dominate engagement: Beauty, fashion, food, travel.**
- **Shopping/discovery is huge:** 63% of SG users have purchased via IG; **68% discover new products/brands there.**
- IG usage skews **25–34 (women-led, 51% women)** — the prime "interested in products & companies" demographic.

## 4. What this means for ObserveCo positioning content

Your instinct to target ObserveCo-positioning angles overlaps with **two of these buckets directly** — and the other four are *adjacent reach*:

- **Money / finance / property** (thewokesalaryman-style takes) — *the closest fit*: high-interest products + companies + "who owns the word" commentary is literally what this bucket is about. **Highest strategic overlap.**
- **Tech / current-affairs** (via CNA/ST audience) — ObserveCo is a tech product; current-affairs framing on AI/agent-observability rides the news audience.
- **Food, travel, fashion** — these are *attention hotspots* but **not** where ObserveCo positioning naturally lives. They're the "big audience, wrong topic" layer. Watch, don't chase.

**The honest strategic conclusion:** The Singapore audience that is both (a) large and (b) already primed for "products + companies + sharp opinion" content is the **money/finance/property + tech/current-affairs** intersection — anchored by accounts like `thewokesalaryman` (the proven SG format: a pointed, well-argued take that a big audience forwards).

**What drives follower growth in SG is a *sharp, argued opinion on something Singaporeans already care about* (money, companies, national issues)** — not trend-chasing per se. `thewokesalaryman` is the best existing proof: near-1M followers on opinionated finance/current-affairs commentary.
