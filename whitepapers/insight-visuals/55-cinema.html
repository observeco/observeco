Cinema is a shrinking pie that one name dominates. Attendance has halved from over 20 million to under 10 million in a decade. The box office is a declining format — streaming (6.9% → 41.1% of households) is the substitute. This is a wall, but a wall that is slowly crumbling. The growth is in live events — concerts, F1, the arts fairs — not in the screen.
