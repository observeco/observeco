Slow structural bleed

Competing on price is structurally dangerous here, not merely a bad strategy. The cost base does not bend, and the market is capped. The business that cuts its price does not get margin relief. It gets a slow, structural bleed: each discount shaves profit, the fixed rent stays exactly where it was, and there is no new volume big enough to make up the difference. You can cut your way to zero customers and still owe the month's rent.
