The childless are not poor. They are the most free-spending adults in the country, on the wrong categories. The money did not disappear when the birth rate fell. It moved sideways.

+2%/yr

Dog population growth

The dog needs walking when nobody is home. The cat needs feeding on the weekend. That is care, and it is bought by exactly the same person: the busy adult with money and no time.

Of the three decisions a pet owner makes — what I feed, who treats my pet, where I buy the stuff — only the veterinary decision is genuinely up for grabs. A name that could own "who treats my pet" in a growing S$120M market is an open slot as the category consolidates. The ownership is still settling.
