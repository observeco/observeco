Every Singaporean has a GP brand in their neighbourhood. This is the one healthcare layer a small operator can actually compete in.

You do not open a hospital. But you can compete in the primary-care layer beneath it. The clinic chains have consolidated — Healthway, Raffles, Parkway Shenton, Fullerton — and these chains own the everyday-doctor word in a way the hospitals cannot. The hospital owns the beds; the clinic owns the neighbourhood. That is the distinction a small healthcare operator needs to understand.
