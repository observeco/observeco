Small Island, Crowded Market — Book 1 · Part Three, Ch 3

Walls and Doors

Some industries are owned by a few giants. Others belong to no one. Know which one you're standing in.

🧱
The Walls
A few names own 80%+ of the money
  • 🏦 DBS, OCBC, UOB — Banking
  • ✈️ Singapore Airlines — ~50% of Changi capacity
  • 📱 Singtel — ~43-50% of mobile
  • 🛒 Shopee, Lazada — ~88% of e-commerce
  • 🏥 IHH, Raffles, Thomson — 80%+ of private beds
  • 📊 PwC, KPMG, Deloitte, EY — ~100% of STI audits
  • 🎰 MBS, RWS — Two casino licences, ever
Don't fight the wall head-on. Find the crack — the niche, the word, the relationship they don't own.
🚪
The Doors
No name owns even a meaningful slice
  • 📚 Tuition — S$1.8bn, no dominant brand
  • 🏠 Senior Care — Growing, leader ~27%
  • 💇 Beauty & Salons — 5,540 establishments, fragmented
  • 👕 Fashion — No domestic label owns a major word
  • 🐕 Pet Care — S$412m, consolidating
  • 🔧 Repair & Laundry — No owner at all
  • 🍽️ Hawker & F&B — Thousands of independents
The door is crowded with interchangeable shops fighting on price. One operator with a clear word takes the share of everyone around them.
The Doors Where Demand Is Growing
👴
S$280M–850M

Addressable care market

75% of care is unpaid. The free layer is breaking. Every dollar that moves from unpaid to paid is a dollar a business can own.

📚
S$1.8bn/yr

Private tuition

No single centre captures even 2% of the market. A parent names a specific centre, and that centre could be yours.

🐕
S$412M

Pet care

Up 12.5% in a year. The money that would have gone to a child went to the dog. The ownership is still settling.

The fragmented industry that is also a growing industry is the rarest, most valuable thing in the map — a category with money and demand, and no name that clearly owns it. The slot is "find a category where the money is growing and no name owns it, verify that, then own the word."
