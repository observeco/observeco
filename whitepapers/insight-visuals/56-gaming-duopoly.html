Two Casino Licences. Ever.

When Singapore finally allowed gambling, it did not open the door — it cut a window. Two licences. One to Las Vegas Sands, one to Genting. The purest case of the state rationing competition into a duopoly on purpose.

Concentration in Singapore was never a market accident. It was a national-security decision. The state did not allow these industries to consolidate — it built them that way, because a country that could not afford failure could not afford duplication. This is a wall you do not enter. But the live events, the concerts, the F1 — that is where the growth is, and that is where a domestic business can serve the visitors the resorts bring in.
