The Three Doors AI Opens for Small Business
Door 1

The niche beside the giant

The Big Four audit every listed company, but the specialist niche they can't profitably touch is now open to a solo advisor with AI agents.

Door 2

The unclaimed word in fragmented categories

A billion dollars in tuition, no dominant brand. AI removes the execution barrier, so the solo who owns a word walks in.

Door 3

The growing demand wave made reachable

Care, convenience, the shrinking household — AI makes these markets scalable for a one-person operator.

The scarce resource is no longer what you can do — AI made that cheap for everyone. It is why anyone should choose you, which, in Singapore, means who you are, who you know, and what word you own. If the only thing you have is the work, then the day the work becomes free, you have nothing.
