National Health Expenditure

Projected to nearly double. Over a quarter of the population will be 65+ by 2030. Every grey hair is a customer.

The moving money splits two ways. It goes to the digitised — online, streamed, delivered — and it goes to the old, to health, care, and the services that compensate for shrinking households. The trick is to be on the side of the country as it actually is, not as it pretended to be.
