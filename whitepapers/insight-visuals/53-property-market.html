The other ~1,000 agencies share the rest. PropNex alone holds over a third.

The developers sit above the agencies — CapitalLand is the largest. The REIT managers own the commercial property layer. This is not a typical arena for a domestic SME, but it is structurally powerful for the landlord and constraining for the tenant. A small business cannot change the real estate structure; it can only choose which part of it to pay — downtown premium or heartland mass. That choice, made consciously, is a positioning decision.
