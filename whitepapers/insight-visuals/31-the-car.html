A household that buys a car is signalling that it has arrived, and committing years of future income to prove it. The car is the one purchase in the whole country where the depreciating asset, the borrowing, and the debt all point the same direction. When a business sees a car, it sees a household with confidence — and very little left over for other big-ticket spending.
