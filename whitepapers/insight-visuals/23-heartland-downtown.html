You do not have to be physically downtown to serve the downtown customer. The premium customer is not loyal to a postal code. They are loyal to a standard. If you can build a reputation for excellence anywhere on the island, the premium customers will travel to you, or they will hire you to travel to them. The geography matters, but the reputation matters more.
