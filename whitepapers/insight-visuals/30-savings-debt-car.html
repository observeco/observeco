The last resort. Legal, regulated, high-cost. The clearest signal of where the money is not.

The car is the most expensive want. A modest Singapore car, all in, can pass a quarter of a million dollars. The COE alone — just the permission — is worth more than the car itself. It is the premium display of the country's wealth, and the one purchase where the depreciating asset and the borrowing point the same direction. A household that buys a car is signalling it has arrived, and committing years of future income to prove it.
