The Johor twinning model

100 projects, 20,000 skilled jobs, special tax incentives. Front office in Singapore, back office in Johor. The practical escape from the domestic cost cap.

The cap is a description of how most small businesses actually operate, not a law of physics. The business that deliberately builds a cross-border or online channel is choosing to leave the capped room. The argument survives because most small businesses do not use these escape routes — they serve the people who can reach the stall. For that majority, the cap is the description of the ground.
