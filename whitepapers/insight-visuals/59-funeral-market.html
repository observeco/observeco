The Funeral Market Has No Dominant Brand

A competition regulator (CCCS) counted more than 800 providers. This is not an assumption — it is a measured finding. The market is genuinely fragmented, genuinely low-concentration, and genuinely no one's.

This is the pattern at its strongest — but it must be read carefully. Two different truths sit here. Verified: the funeral market genuinely has no dominant brand (CCCS counted 800+ providers, that is measured, not assumed). Unverified: for repair, laundry, and watch servicing there is simply no published share data — which is a gap in the map, not proof the field is open. Both are opportunities to investigate, but the first is a finding and the second is an absence of evidence.
