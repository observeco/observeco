Four Names Sign Every Listed Company's Accounts

PwC, KPMG, Deloitte, EY — together, essentially 100% of the STI audit market. The same four names, year after year. A board that switches firms is a story.

The five largest single audit bills made up nearly half of PwC's pool.

The Big Four's grip is total and growing. This is a wall you do not enter. But the specialist niches they can't profitably touch — a niche tax structure, a single-industry compliance question, a bespoke advisory for one type of foreign investor — those are the cracks. The solo advisor with AI agents can field the full execution of a firm and put it behind one sharp word: "the specialist in this niche."
