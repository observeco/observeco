Consumer stream: referral is everything

Fewer than 1 in 4 F&B businesses survive 5 years. The consumer stream rewards the owner who has picked one rung of the ladder and does that one thing better than the street around it.

S$97B

Government stream: the door is compliance

The state funds care, health, education. Getting in takes work — standards, audits, qualifications. But once you're in, it's the most defensible stream a small business can enter.

800

B2B stream: the pyramid

800 big companies sit at the top. Below them, tier-one suppliers. Below that, tier-two and tier-three — where small businesses live. The path: get one small job, do it perfectly.

1.23M

Foreign worker stream: the tap you don't control

Real money, but sensitive to policy. The number of work passes is a tool of national policy. Build into it, but don't let it be your only water.

You cannot stand in all five streams at once. Each stream asks for a different customer, a different product, a different temperament. The scarce thing is not the money. The scarce thing is the owner who has chosen a stream and built a business that fits it. Choose your stream, and you have already done what most of your competitors will never manage.
