Per capita GDP

But this average hides the three-engine split. The global worker's output is 15× the domestic worker's. The headline is not the ground.

The headline says the economy is booming. And the numbers say the headlines are right. But the problem is the decade, not the good years. Between 2016 and 2024, labour productivity grew 2.5%/yr while real income grew only 1.3%/yr. The economy got more productive; the people who work in it did not get the gain in proportion.
