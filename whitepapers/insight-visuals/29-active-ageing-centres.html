The silver screen

Seniors are moving onto the screens

Streaming, video calls, social media — filling the hours that work used to fill. That is a market, not a lonely fact about old people.

The whole silver economy

Built on one giant fact

A retired person has more hours in a day than anyone else in the country. Every hour is a slot waiting to be filled — monitoring, meals, companionship, classes.

A small business in care, health, or ageing-in-place is standing in a current the state has chosen to strengthen, not riding a current that happens to be moving. The state is writing the cheques, building the infrastructure, and creating the market. The question is not whether the market will grow — it is whether you will be one of the few who has built a boat on that river before the water rises.
