What everyone thinks

"Small businesses struggle because they're small"

If that were true, nothing you did would close the gap. You'd be stuck. The data says the opposite.

What the data actually says

"Small businesses struggle because they're undifferentiated"

The gap between the best and worst small firms is 7 to 1. That gap is almost entirely about position and execution — not size. A positioned 2-person business can out-earn a generic 10-person one.

If the gap were about size, you would be stuck — nothing you did would close it. But it is about how you run the business. And that is a problem you can actually do something about. The question is not "how do I get bigger?" — it is "how do I become the best small firm in my space?"
