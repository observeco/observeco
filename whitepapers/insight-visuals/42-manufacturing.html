A domestic SME does not compete in manufacturing; it competes around it — as a supplier, a service provider, a niche player in a cluster the giants ignore. Manufacturing is dominated by multinationals and state-linked firms, is capital-intensive, and its value is concentrated in a small number of large firms. The data is excellent. The barriers to entry are brutal.
