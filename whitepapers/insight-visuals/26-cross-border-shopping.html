The marketplace is already won. But the one thing a foreign seller cannot do, a local seller can. The race is not to sell online. The race is to sell online to someone a foreign seller cannot serve — the neighbour who needs it today, the customer who wants to see it first, the person who trusts a name their friend mentioned. That is the local advantage the platforms cannot replicate.
