Small Island, Crowded Market — Book 1 · Part Two, Ch 3

The Care Economy Is Not What the Headlines Say

The "silver economy" headline is $72 billion. The real market a business can own is a few hundred million. Here's why that's actually better.

📰
What the headlines say
US$72 billion
"Silver economy" — total spending power of every person over 60, including groceries, housing, electricity. A headline, not a market.
💰
What a business can actually own
S$280M – S$850M
The paid layer of care for ~52,000 seniors with moderate-to-severe disability. Home care, day care, residential care, meals, safety monitoring. Growing every year.
🏠
75%

of care is unpaid

Family members — the daughter rushing across town with food. The free layer is breaking. Every dollar that stops being free moves into a market a business can own.

👩‍👧
1 in 4

working adults will be sandwiched by 2030

Caring for an ageing parent and a child, with their own working life between. 95% say it's their responsibility. The capacity to actually do it has collapsed.

👵
88,000

seniors live alone

More than double since 2015. Each one buying everything — meals, safety, transport, monitoring — because there's nobody at home to do it.

💵
S$600/mo

Home Caregiving Grant (from Apr 2026)

The state is writing cheques to make sure the market exists. A small business in care is standing in a current the state has chosen to strengthen.

👩‍💼

The customer is not the senior. It's the daughter.

The 45-year-old who leaves her office at 6pm, collects her child, then drives across town with food for her mother. She wants to be a good daughter. She simply has no hours left. Every paid care service exists to buy back her time.

The paid layer is small, but the unpaid layer is where the growth is. The daughter cannot keep doing it — she's out of hours. As the family layer fails, the care moves from unpaid to paid. That is the wave, and it is still mostly ahead of us. The river is not an ocean, but it flows through every neighbourhood, and almost nobody has built a reliable boat on it yet.
