Transport — The National Champions

SIA carries half of Changi's passengers. ComfortDelGro holds two-thirds of taxis. SBS Transit runs three-fifths of buses. The state built these walls on purpose.

✈️
Singapore Airlines + Scoot
~50% of Changi
The flag carrier and its low-cost arm. S$19B revenue. Record 42.4M passengers in 2024/25. A wing-borne flag to carry a tiny island's exports and reputation to the world.
🚕
ComfortDelGro
~64% of taxi market
The largest land-transport operator. Held the dominant position as the market shrank from a 28,736-taxi peak in 2014. The taxi is a shrinking pie that one name dominates.

One national operator for the port, one for the rail, one for the bus, one for the air. These are walls built by national-security decision, not market accident. A small business does not compete with them — it serves the people they employ and the passengers they carry.
