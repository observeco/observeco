Services Generate Over 70% of Singapore's GDP

The goods-producing industries — manufacturing, construction, utilities — generate the rest. This is the three-engine structure, now visible at the level of industries.

The Three Engines, Visible at Industry Level
Global Engine

Builds and trades with the world

Manufacturing, finance, wholesale trade. Capital-intensive. Export-oriented. Dominated by multinationals. Where the value is — but not where most small businesses live.

Government Engine

The state runs the country

Public administration, education, health. Funded by tax and the state's balance sheet. The state's own workshops. Not a market — a service.

Domestic Layer

Serves people where they live

Retail, food & beverage, accommodation, personal services. Where most Singaporeans work. Dense, capped, competitive. Won by position, not price.

Think of it as the machine room versus the shop front versus the state's own workshops. The engines make money for the whole country; the domestic layer takes that money from the people who hold it. They are not just separate places — they are separate industries, with entirely different economics.
