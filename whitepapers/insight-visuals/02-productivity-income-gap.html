Every year, the country gets better at making things — nearly twice as fast as the people who do the making are getting paid for it. More value is being created, and a smaller slice of it is being paid out as wages. For a small business owner, that is the water you are swimming in: a capped domestic market where the margin to absorb a price war is structurally thin.
