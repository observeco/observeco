One national operator for the telecom — the state's instinct was never to have three grid operators. Three would have been a luxury a small island could not afford; one, given a licence and a moat, was a necessity it could not live without. This is a wall you do not enter. But the service layer around it — the plans, the bundling, the customer experience — that is where a small business can serve the people these companies employ.
