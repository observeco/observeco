The Self-Made Brands — Grew Big Without a State Licence
Charles & Keith
Two brothers. Single shop in 1996. Global footwear label. No state parent.
Banyan Tree
Founded on a bold idea. Not a state grant. Global hotel brand.
Grab / Shopee / Carousell
Grew from nothing in the digital era. No licence. No state link.

Every single one of these became big in a fragmented, open category — footwear, hospitality, bakery, and then the internet. None of them tried to out-build DBS or out-scale Singapore Airlines. They won a word in a category the state had left open, and they grew it until the word was the whole category. The state's champions are the norm; the self-made brand is the exception that proves how hard it is. But the exception also proves it is possible.
