Do not chase the big numbers. Wholesale, manufacturing, finance are the engine's territory — capital, scale, and corporate dominance make them hostile to a typical domestic SME. The domestic-layer industries are small in GDP terms, but they are where the people are, where the premium demand flows, and where differentiation actually decides who survives.
