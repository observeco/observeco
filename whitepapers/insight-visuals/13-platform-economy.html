A platform is a shelf, not a brand. When a shopper types a search into Shopee, they get a grid of near-identical products and the cheapest wins. There is no brand in that grid, only listings. The platform is where you get found; the brand is what you must become. The main defence is to build a direct relationship with your customers, so the platform is not the only way they can reach you.
