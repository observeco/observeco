The helper is the person who makes the whole arrangement hold.

She buys the groceries. She takes the children to the playground. She cooks the meals.

In practical terms, the helper is often the person in the household who actually goes to the shops and actually decides, at the shelf, what gets bought. If you run a small grocery or a neighbourhood provision shop, the helper is one of your most important customers — and she is often under-appreciated in the way owners think about their customer base.

Both parents work

The dual-income family only exists because of her

In Singapore, when both parents work and there is no extended family around, the helper is the person who makes the whole arrangement hold. She is what puts both salaries into the spending pool that reaches your shop.

She remembers which stall gives good service

Your most loyal customer may be invisible

She is the one who goes back every week. She is the one who remembers which stall gives good service. Treat her as a proper customer, not as an afterthought.

The foreign-worker and helper payroll is real money, but it is money that is sensitive to policy. The number of work passes the government issues is a tool of national policy, and it moves up and down with the economy. A business that builds its whole model on this current is building on a base that can be tightened. Build into it, but do not let it be your only water.
