Singapore is a top-two AI nation and still a bottom-half AI small-business economy. That gap between the national muscle and the small-firm reality is the single most important fact: it means the infrastructure, the talent, the government money are all in place, and the small business that moves now is riding a wave the country is actively building, not waiting for it. The field being this empty is the whole point.
