## 4. What full source verification requires (the honest scope)

~123,000 words cannot be line-verified in one session. Full verification is a **multi-session discipline**:

1. **Every brand fact** — founding date, founder name, store count, revenue, market share, key product — against the company's own site + an independent source (Wikipedia or established press). ~20 brands × 4-6 facts each.
2. **Every market figure** — GDP, FDI, CPF, workforce, AI adoption, retail/F&B survival, referral rates — against SingStat/MTI/MOM primary releases (some already verified this session).
3. **Every LKY/nation-branding claim** — against History Today, the National Archives, and reputable biographies.
4. **Every "confidence-labeled Directional" figure** — either find a source or downgrade/remove the precision (e.g., a market share stated as a single number when the source gives a range).

**Recommendation:** do this as a tracked checklist before publication, brand-by-brand and figure-by-figure. I can run it, but it needs its own sessions (or delegation) to do properly — not a single pass.

## 5. Book-writing standards review — what the manuscripts are missing

Beyond legal/IP and accuracy, a proper trade book needs the following. **None of these are currently in the manuscripts.**

### Front matter (before Part One)
- **Title page** (title, subtitle, author/credits) — currently the title/credit lives in a working header, not a real title page.
- **Copyright page** — required: © year, publisher/imprint, ISBN, "All rights reserved" notice, the disclaimer that it's not professional/legal advice, and the attribution line for Ries/Trout/Gu concepts (credit, not endorsement).
- **Dedication / epigraph** (optional but standard for a trade book).
- **Table of contents** — with parts and chapters numbered, so readers can navigate.
- **Foreword / Preface / Introduction** — the books open into Part One with no framing front-matter.

### Back matter (after the final chapter)
- **Appendix / Glossary** — a plain-language glossary of the acronyms and terms (CPF, SME, OPC, SSIC, etc.) would serve the reader; the acronyms are glossed inline but a consolidated glossary is a book-standard addition.
- **Sources / Bibliography** — a consolidated, chapter-keyed bibliography. Currently sources are inline confidence notes; a proper book has a Sources section a reader can look up.
- **Acknowledgements** (optional).
- **About the author / colophon** (optional).

### Structure & navigation standards
- **Consistent chapter numbering and headings** — the books use `# PART X` and `##` sub-heads but chapters are not numbered sequentially (e.g., "PART TWO-A" breaks the sequence). A reader expects numbered chapters.
- **A consistent "book / part / chapter / section" terminology** — this was partially addressed, but a final sweep should ensure every structural reference uses the same word for the same level.
- **Running heads / headers** in the rendered PDF — the page header should carry the book title and chapter.
- **Page numbers** — present only after rendering.

### Editing & craft standards
- **Manuscript hygiene** — the working-document headers ("ObserveCo Consulting · Singapore · A5 book manuscript") must be stripped; they are not book content.
- **Style sheet / house style** — a single style guide for numerals ($ vs S$, percentages, punctuation, heading case) applied consistently. Currently inconsistent in places (e.g., "per cent" vs "%", "S$" vs "$").
- **Consistency pass** — every acronym glossed at first use (already a locked rule), every recurring term used the same way.

### Production standards (for the eventual PDF/EPUB)
- **ISBN registration** (one per book).
- **Typesetting spec** — the A5 format, margins, font, and page target are set; the render must apply them consistently.
- **Cover design** — front cover, spine, back cover with blurb.
- **Metadata** — EPUB metadata (title, author, language, ISBN, rights) for distribution.
- **Digital rights** — a decision on DRM and the copyright/licence statement.
- **Accessibility** — alt-text on any images, reflowable text in EPUB.

### Pre-publication checklist (the full gate)
1. Source-verify every figure (multi-session, §4).
2. Correct R1 (Grab) and R2 (GDP growth).
3. Strip all working-document headers; add real front matter.
4. Add back matter (glossary, bibliography, sources).
5. Standardise chapter numbering; fix "PART TWO-A" sequencing.
6. House-style pass (numerals, currency, punctuation).
7. IPOS + USPTO title search.
8. Final IP risk re-scan (Ries/Trout/Gu list content) — the one thing that could reintroduce risk.
9. Singapore-qualified IP/defamation lawyer review if authoritative sign-off wanted.
10. ISBN, cover, typesetting, metadata, then render.
