## (a) E-COMMERCE / ONLINE RETAIL — PLATFORM GMV
Top 4 platforms = ~100% of Singapore *platform* GMV (2025, Momentum Works via The Straits Times).
| Rank | Brand | Share | Position | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Shopee** (Sea Ltd, SG-listed) | **52%** | #1 leader | The Straits Times / Momentum Works, Apr 14 2026 — Singapore platform GMV US$5.9bn 2025, +21% YoY; "Shopee 52%, Lazada 36%, Amazon & TikTok Shop 6% each" |
| 2 | **Lazada** (Alibaba) | **36%** | #2 | Same ST/Momentum Works report |
| 3 | **Amazon** | **6%** | Tied #3 | Same ST/Momentum Works report |
| 3 | **TikTok Shop** (ByteDance) | **6%** | Tied #3 | Same ST/Momentum Works report |

- Corroborating/2022 baseline: **Shopee ~53%, Lazada ~35%** (Cube Asia, "Singapore E-commerce Market 2025," Jan 2025; cited in WhiteBox, 2026).
- Regional context: Shopee, Lazada + TikTok/Tokopedia = **98.8% of SEA platform GMV 2025** (Momentum Works, 2026). Shopee #1 in all six markets.
- Cross-border dominant: 62% of SG cross-border e-commerce from China (PPRO via Cube Asia).
- **Takeaway:** Shopee + Lazada alone ≈ **88%** of Singapore platform e-commerce spend — these two capture the ~80% threshold by themselves; adding TikTok & Amazon reaches ~100%.

## Summary / gaps
- **(a) E-commerce:** HIGH confidence — Shopee+Lazada ≈ 88% of platform GMV; the "top set" is crisp & citable.
- **(b) Personal services:** LOW data availability — fragmented; only product-channel (L'Oréal ~10.4%; pharmacy chains ~80%) and corporate-vet (Mars/Mount Pleasant) signals exist. No legitimate ~80% brand set can be sourced.
- **(c) Fashion:** MEDIUM — YouGov consideration shares for int'l leaders (Uniqlo dominant); SHEIN rising for women/Gen Z; Love, Bonito the flagship domestic brand; no strict spend-share %.

**Do not use US/global share figures** (e.g. Grand View, Fact.MR, WWD global lists) — they are not Singapore-specific and violate the brief.
