## Overall honesty summary
1. **No brand in SSIC S has published, audited market share** — including Kimage, Piing, WashSquad, Singapore Casket, Ang Chin Moh. This is the least-measured industry (≈1.1% of GDP).
2. **CCCS is the one authoritative exception:** it found funeral services fragmented with no high concentration and >800 providers — the closest thing to an official market-structure statement in this sector.
3. Only proxy indicators exist: outlet counts (Kimage=8, AUTOBAC=2), heritage/recognition (Ang Chin Moh since 1912; Truefitt 1805), and qualitative "leader" claims (Leekka "Korea #1"; Song Kwang "largest fleet") — none is a share figure.
4. Foreign brands are meaningful only in (i) beauty/hair (Leekka/韩国), (ii) auto service (AUTOBAC, Japan), (iii) watch OEM servicing (Swatch Group), and (iv) funeral (Nirvana, Malaysia). Everything else is domestic fragmentation.
