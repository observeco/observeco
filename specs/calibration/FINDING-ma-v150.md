## The new regression, and what it reveals

**Gong Cha Singapore: MA me 1 → Sean 4 (+3), now the single worst case.**

Gong Cha **shut all 29 Singapore outlets in October 2025**. My v1.5.0 rule says a low score
needs positive evidence of non-retrieval, and "ceased" is exactly that evidence — so it
scored 1. **Sean scores 4.**

**This is not obviously an error on his part.** Brand memory PERSISTS after closure: buyers
still retrieve Gong Cha when they think of bubble tea, even though they can no longer buy
it. So the rule "ceased → not retrieved" is wrong for `mental_advantage` specifically —
closure destroys REACH (a DR fact, where Sean agreed the closed outlet is a 2) but does not
immediately destroy MEMORY (an MA fact).

**The general lesson:** the same physical fact — "this business has closed" — has opposite
implications for different dimensions. DR: reach is gone. MA: retrieval persists.
**A corroboration rule must therefore be dimension-specific, not global.** I had been
building one shared "physical evidence" doctrine across dimensions; this case shows that is
too blunt.

## The construct question I will not try to word my way out of

Sean scores **5** for Courts, Don Don Donki, Harvey Norman and Cold Storage. My level 5
reads *"Owns the segment… rivals are not close."* These businesses demonstrably **have**
close rivals — that is what makes them mass-market. Their prices converge within 3–8%.

So one of these must be true, and only Sean can say which:

1. **His 5 means "dominant presence in the segment"**, not "uncontested" → my level 5 is
   mis-worded and should be re-anchored to presence, not exclusivity.
2. **His 5 is a level-4 reading** with a systematically higher personal anchor → calibration,
   fixable by re-anchoring.
3. **He is grading the brand's standing absolutely**, not retrieval relative to rivals → a
   construct difference in the frame itself.

I have now re-worded MA **twice** (v1.5.0 plus the earlier size-anchoring amendment) and
moved three of four cases by exactly one point. **A third re-wording is the wrong move.**
This is the question Test 3 exists to answer, and it should be answered before any further
MA iteration.
