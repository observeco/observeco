## The definition

**How hard is this position to REPLICATE?** What would a well-resourced challenger have to assemble
to do the same thing and win on execution? Name the mechanism:

| 1 | No barrier — nothing to assemble; anyone could do it immediately |
| 2 | Shallow — only a claimed difference, copyable in weeks |
| 3 | Replicable — real work required, but nothing obstructs beyond the cost of doing it |
| 4 | Protected — a genuine mechanism (IP, capital intensity, network control, scale economics, switching costs, accumulated asset) |
| 5 | Durable — would take a well-resourced challenger a decade or more |
| 6 | Compounding — several mechanisms reinforce each other |

**Then subtract one level if a rival could bypass the position entirely** and take significant share
another way (a different channel, a cheaper format, an importer undercutting). A marginal alternative
changes nothing.
