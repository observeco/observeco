## 1. What They Do

Likeable Pets is a **mobile pet grooming service** operating island-wide in Singapore. They bring professional grooming to the customer's home — no salon visits, no waiting, no transporting pets.

**Services offered:**

| Service | What's included | Dog Pricing | Cat Pricing |
|---------|----------------|-------------|-------------|
| **Basic Grooming** | Nail clipping, ear cleaning, sanitary clip, paw pad shave, bath & fluff | $80–$150 (S/M/L/XL) | $120–$130 (short/long hair) |
| **Full Grooming** | Everything in Basic + trimming/styling OR deshedding | $120–$180 (S/M/L/XL) | $150–$160 (short/long hair) |
| **Transport fee** | Not included in above | Starts from $40 (location-dependent) | Same |

**Pricing note:** Prices are a starting guide. Actual rates vary by breed, coat type, fur length, and thickness. They encourage WhatsApp for an indicative quote.

**Operating hours:** Daily, 10am–6pm (last appointment 4:30pm)
**Payment:** PayNow, Paylah, or cash (on-site after session)

## 3. Positioning

**Tagline:** *"Professional pet grooming at your door step"*

**Positioning statement (inferred):** The stress-free, convenient alternative to pet salons — certified groomers who come to your home, treat your pet with one-on-one attention, and leave your space clean.

**Target customer:** Singapore pet owners who:
- Don't have time for salon visits (waiting, transport, traffic)
- Have anxious/elderly pets that stress in unfamiliar environments
- Want to avoid exposure to other animals (contagious diseases, parasites, barking)
- Value convenience and are willing to pay a premium for it ($80–$180 + $40 transport)

**Tone of voice:** Warm, caring, pet-first. The "About" page is written from personal experience — the founder couldn't find a gentle groomer for their own pet, so they learned grooming themselves. This origin story is their brand anchor.

## 4. Unique Differentiators

| Differentiator | Detail | Why it matters |
|---------------|--------|----------------|
| **Mobile/home service** | Groomer comes to your home with all equipment | Eliminates transport stress, waiting time, traffic |
| **One-on-one attention** | No other animals present | Safer for anxious pets, no disease exposure, no barking stress |
| **Certified groomers** | SKC, ISFC, grooming academy certified | Trust signal — not a side hustle, trained professionals |
| **Clean environment** | Grooming in the pet's own home | Familiar scents/sounds reduce anxiety; no kennel cough risk |
| **Quality holistic products** | Organic, natural, vet-endorsed brands | Gentler on skin/coat, no harsh chemicals |
| **Photo-based cut preference** | Show a photo of desired cut | Avoids "puppy cut" ambiguity — common pain point |
| **Post-grooming cleanup** | Groomer clears the area before leaving | Zero mess for the owner |

## 8. Competitive Landscape (Inferred)

The mobile pet grooming space in Singapore has several players. Likeable Pets differentiates on:

1. **Certification transparency** — they name their groomers, show certificates, list years of experience
2. **Cat specialisation** — Chelsea is a dedicated cat groomer (ISFC certified), which is rarer than dog grooming
3. **Review volume** — 505 Google reviews is strong for a local service business
4. **Award badges** — multiple "Best in Singapore" type awards create trust

**Likely competitors:** Other mobile groomers in Singapore (Waggle, Pawfect, etc.) — but Likeable Pets' review volume and certification transparency give them an edge.
