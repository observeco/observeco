Concept 1 · Breathing Glow

Light through a window — calm, alive, never moving.

A single soft teal glow slowly breathes in opacity. Nothing travels, so nothing reads as a panel. The eye registers warmth, not motion.

Zero translation · 8s pulse · most calm
Concept 2 · Grain + Hue Drift

Premium paper texture, alive with a slow colour breath.

A fine film-grain gives tactility; the teal tint drifts over 30 seconds. The eye can't track a colour shift as motion — it just feels alive.

Almost no motion · 30s hue · safest
Concept 3 · Soft Orbs

Depth-of-field light, drifting like bokeh.

Three huge, heavily-blurred teal circles crawl diagonally. The blur is what separates this from the old sharp dots — it reads as lens light, not particles.

3 blurred orbs · 26–38s · depth
Concept 4 · Light Sweep

A sheen moving across a surface — horizontal, not a panel.

One wide, very soft band of light sweeps horizontally across the page. The direction change reframes it as light on a surface, not a panel sliding down.

1 wide band · 15s sweep · horizontal
Concept 5 · Contour Drift

Faint terrain lines, slowly undulating.

Three wavy contour lines barely move — the pattern subtly undulates. Ties to the Singapore-map motif; reads as texture, not a moving object.

3 wavy lines · slow phase · map motif
