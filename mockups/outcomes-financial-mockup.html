Three bounded outcomes

Three different endings, not three stages.

Which one you land on depends on how far that one fact can travel. The numbers below are the same story told in money — as the area grows, revenue grows, but margin compresses and risk rises.

Narrow hold

You own 100% of a small market. Nobody fights you for it, so it stays profitable — and it only stays yours if you keep it small enough to defend.
