The gap is leverage, and the one lever a small business can build is brand — a name people trust. You cannot build the world or borrow capital overnight. But a name that others risk their own reputation vouching for is exactly what turns the median hour into the professional's. The same mechanism that prices the PM's cheque is the one a lone operator is already standing inside.
