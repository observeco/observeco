The pattern: Grab wins where it extends an existing high-frequency habit (food → groceries, rides → payments). It struggles where it jumps into a low-frequency, high-commitment category (transit was discontinued; travel keeps relaunching). A car is the lowest-frequency, highest-commitment purchase of all.
