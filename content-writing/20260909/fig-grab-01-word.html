Grab's trust is built on low-commitment, high-frequency transactions — your ride shows up, your food arrives. A car is a high-commitment, low-frequency purchase (S$11,800–S$249,988, bought once every several years). The mental association is weak, and sgcarmart already owns the word for "used-car marketplace."
