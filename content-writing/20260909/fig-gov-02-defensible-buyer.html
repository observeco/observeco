The government doesn't want to own every eldercare centre. It wants them to exist, so it pays for them and expects the sector to provide them. A small operator who can run a reliable programme and meet the standards is a provider the state actively needs. Government-funded demand doesn't vanish in a downturn — it's the most defensible stream a small business can enter.
