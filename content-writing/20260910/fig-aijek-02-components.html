What Actually Holds a Garment Together — and What Happens When It's Buried

A dress is a system. The claim "returns to the soil" is only as strong as the worst component.

The FTC Green Guides require "biodegradable" to apply to the product as a whole — not a material within it. A cotton dress sewn with polyester thread is not a biodegradable product. The garden test buried fabrics, not garments — and the components that hold a garment together are exactly the ones that don't decompose.
