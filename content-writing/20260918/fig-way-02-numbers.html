What is actually on the road today, and what arrives when

70,000 Drivers. About 20 Robotaxis. And a Government Saying It Will Not Go Faster Than the Retraining.

The gap between the announcement and the road is the whole story. Read the numbers together and the "2028 launch" stops looking like a threat.

On the road now — human
70,000+

Taxi and private-hire drivers in Singapore today. Point-to-point transport is still a people business.

On the road now — autonomous
~20

Autonomous vehicles on Singapore roads, mostly in trials. A rounding error against the manned fleet.

The roadmap, in the order it happens

Next few months
Cars arrive
All-electric Jaguar I-PACE fleet lands. Not carrying passengers.
2027
Manual driving
Trained specialists drive to map local roads, monsoon rain, and road geometry.
2028
Commercial service
Fully driverless ride-hailing opens to the public through the Waymo app.
