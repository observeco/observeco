Ten years, and the same city on both ends

Singapore Was First in the World at This. In 2028 It Will Be a Customer.

The country that ran the first on-demand driverless taxi trial is now the market being sold to. The technology did not change hands. The position did.

Aug 2016

First in the world

nuTonomy, an MIT spin-off, starts on-demand driverless taxis in one-north. Singapore becomes the first country with a public robotaxi trial, ahead of Uber.

➜
2028

Third operator, foreign-owned

Waymo — an Alphabet subsidiary — aims to launch commercial driverless ride-hailing here. Arriving behind Grab and ComfortDelGro.

2014
Testing began
Singapore has been trialling AVs since 2014.
2016
one-north testbed
The on-road testbed that made the world-first possible.
2017
The pioneer was acquired
nuTonomy absorbed by Delphi. The head start did not become ownership.
