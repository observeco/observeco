Thirteen use cases, in the order they arrive

What You Can Build With Four-Cent Decisions

Every one is the same shape: a job that was too expensive to do properly, so it was done badly or not at all.

Group 1 — you could start today
Each one is a job that currently gets sampled because reading everything costs too much.
1. Check an agent's work, every step
Did it read the right file? Is this action risky? Hoping becomes knowing.
2. Read the whole pile, not a sample
Contracts, logs, reviews, invoices, notes, survey answers.
3. Routing
"Who handles this?" — better than a rules list, because it judges.
4. Spotting the attack
Prompt injection, odd account behaviour. A yes/no call.
5. Are these the same thing?
Duplicate customers, relevant results, repeat comments.
Group 2 — only works because it's instant
Under a tenth of a second is the line where AI stops being something you wait for.
6. AI inside the moment
A voice that decides whether to interrupt. Enemies that react. A form that adapts as you type.
7. Flipping which one is the exception
The model makes the soft call. The code keeps the authority.
8. Two lanes instead of one
Cheap decider first, hard cases up to the big model. Caching, for judgment.
9. The line between acting and asking
Above the confidence line it acts alone. Below it, a person decides. The dial is tunable.
Group 3 — nobody has started
The part I would bet on, and the part that answers the question nobody can answer yet.
10. Every message against the rulebook
Banks and insurers sample today. Now every message is checked, scored, and auditable.
11. Checking the confidence numbers
Is it right 90 times out of 100? That drifts. Nobody owns this job yet.
12. Quality as a live number
Score every output as it happens, not once a quarter.
13. Things nobody has named yet
Every big price drop makes never-worth-it decisions worth making. Nobody planned 2FA either.
Sources: TypeSafe AI launch post, 15 Sep 2026 (primitives, pricing, latency, the calibration quote). Use-case groupings are applied analysis, not vendor claims. Moderate · Low-Moderate for group 3
