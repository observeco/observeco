What won at home, and what wins here

Matsuya Did Not Lose in Japan. It Just Brought the Wrong Scorecard.

Third place at home is not a defect. But the axis that decides the Japanese market is not the axis that is already decided in Singapore.

The axis in Japan

Volume and price, on a counter seat

  • Three chains holding ~5,000+ stores, most open 24/7
  • Fought bowl-by-bowl on price — a regular bowl near ¥400–600
  • Matsuya's edge is the set meal: rice, soup, a side, more vegetables
➜
The axis already set in Singapore

Halal certification and the mall food court

  • About 15–16% of residents are Muslim — a market the chains must serve to compete
  • Sukiya and Yoshinoya hold MUIS certification across their outlets
  • Japanese casual chains live or die on footfall in a mall, at S$6–9 a bowl
