# Educational Article: "Why This Maybank Ad Fails — and the Positioning Fix"

## Working title options
- "The Beige Ad: What a Maybank Wealth Ad Gets Wrong (and the Correct Positioning)"
- "A Refined Perspective is Not a Position: A Positioning Autopsy of a Maybank Ad"
- "Why 'A Refined Perspective' Doesn't Sell — a Positioning Deconstruction"

## The hook (fear/discovery)
Open with the uncomfortable truth: most "wealth management" advertising is interchangeable. Then prove it — the reader has seen this exact ad with a dozen different logos. Then: "I'm going to show you why, using an actual Maybank ad, and then rebuild it correctly."

## The structure (educational, demonstrates application)

### 1. The ad (what it actually says)
Present the extracted copy faithfully. Admit it's an ad in the wild (iPhone photo), not a studio concept. This builds honesty + credibility.

### 2. The theory in one page — what positioning actually is
- Ries & Trout: positioning is owning a spot in the prospect's mind against named competitors
- The three tests (specific customer/job, one thing you win on + competitor unseated, verbal+visual categories agree)
- Key: position ≠ features, position = distinct place vs competition

### 3. The autopsy — why this ad fails (the five failures)
Failure by failure, each tied to a theory principle and a line from the ad.
- Missing customer/job
- No contrast/tension
- Self-description vs benefit
- Visual-verbal category mismatch
- CTA no cost-reversal

### 4. The research — what Maybank actually has
Bring in the subagent-verified facts: ASEAN network, Islamic bank dominance, heritage, integrated ecosystem. Show the gap between what the ad said and what the bank owns.

### 5. The competitor map — where the whitespace is
Table: DBS/UOB/OCBC/Citi/HSBC/StanChart/CIMB/RHB positions. Show that the ASEAN-corridor + Islamic-wealth space is open.

### 6. The correct approach
The positioning sentence. The wedge. How every redesign element traces to it.

### 7. The rebuild (Spectrum's deliverable)
The redesigned ad — visually, copy, CTA. Show theory → applied.

### 8. The proof it works
Why the redesign passes the three tests the original failed.

## Notes on format
- Written in first person ("I keep seeing ads like this")
- Verifiable Maybank facts, marked UNVERIFIED where subagent couldn't confirm
- The competitor table is the artifact-first element (devs/audience share tables)
- Ends on the proof, not a slogan (humanizer rule: no fake-profound kickers)
