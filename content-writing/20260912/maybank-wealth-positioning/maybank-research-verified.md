# Verified Research Notes — Maybank Wealth Ad Analysis

## Maybank — verified directly from maybank.com home page + Wikipedia
- **Brand purpose tagline:** "Humanising Financial Services" (Maybank's own words on maybank.com)
- **Purpose statement:** "We make it our business to be the financial enabler that supports your financial aspirations and ambitions, by equipping you with easily accessible and intuitive financial facilities and solutions. All with a personal touch."
- **Wealth tiers (nav):** Maybank Private, Maybank Premier, Maybank Privilege
- **Wealth content themes on home page:** Will/Wasiat, legacy planning, "Why Will/Wasiat Is Not Just for the Wealthy" — i.e. inclusive, human-scale wealth stories
- **Founded:** 31 May 1960, Kuala Lumpur, by Singaporean tycoon Khoo Teck Puat
- **Scope:** universal bank, home markets Malaysia, Singapore, Indonesia; network across all 10 ASEAN nations + key Asian centres + global financial centres; 2,600 retail branches; 43,000+ employees
- **Scale:** Malaysia's largest bank by market cap and total assets; one of the largest in SE Asia; total assets exceeding US$203B (2019); largest public listed company on Bursa Malaysia
- **Islamic:** Maybank Islamic is the largest Islamic bank in ASEAN and Malaysia by assets; named 2020's Global Islamic Bank of the Year by The Banker
- **Group entities:** Maybank Securities, Maybank Asset Management, Maybank Investment Bank, Etiqa Insurance & Takaful, Maybank Trustees

## The whitespace / gap
- DBS/OCBC/UOB all claim "ASEAN" or "Asia" connectivity but are Singapore-centric banks reaching out.
- Citi/HSBC/StanChart are global banks thin on ASEAN ground; their "regional" is a feature, not home.
- **Nobody owns the position "We ARE ASEAN — our home, not our foreign market" with the region's dominant Islamic wealth capability.** Maybank is the ASEAN-native with the deepest real footprint across all 10 nations + the largest Islamic bank in ASEAN. That is the open, defensible position.

## Ad under analysis (OCR-verified)

- Body 1: "Tap into insights from across ASIA, with expert perspectives on market trends and opportunities."
- Sub-head: "A SELECTION CURATED FOR YOU"
- Body 2: "Access a world of investment opportunities with Maybank Securities, curated around your goals."
- CTA: "SPEAK WITH US"
- Palette: cream/off-white + brown/charcoal + warm gold. Old-wealth heritage visual language. No teal/blue anywhere.
