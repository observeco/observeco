The choice — falling tide, rising tide

The Current Is Running Somewhere. Are You Standing In It?

Switzerland's watch industry is one example; Singapore's map is another. Both are answers to the same question a business owner should ask first.

Falling tide

Swiss watch exports

  • −42% volume in nine years (25.4M → 14.6M pieces)
  • Price-raise strategy saved only Rolex, Patek, Cartier
  • Mid-market crushed — fewer than 1 in 4 mid-price players thrive
  • Substitutes won: resale market, Japanese, Chinese contenders
  • Skill was not the problem. The current was.
Rising tides (Singapore, numbered)

Ageing · care · health · small-business tech

  • 20.7% of residents 65+ — and rising (13.1% in 2015)
  • 1.49M singles — one person buys everything alone
  • TFR 0.87 — fewer kids, but expensively raised
  • 48-pt SME tech adoption gap — closing now
  • Health & social structure: 1/10 brutal — most open in the economy

In a falling tide, an excellent position is a lifeboat — it means you sink last. In a rising tide, a merely competent position earns more than a brilliant one fighting the current. Choose the tide first. Then choose your boat.
