Swiss Watch Exports: The Tide Went Out

From the Federation of the Swiss Watch Industry's own export line — 25.4 million timepieces in 2016, 14.6 million in 2025. Volume cut by 42% in nine years.

The industry's response was to raise prices — and that strategy protected only the cult brands (Rolex, Patek Philippe, Cartier). Mid-market players and their suppliers were hit hardest. Skill was not the problem; the current was.
