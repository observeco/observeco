When the Gym Closes, You Are Not a Customer. You Are an Unsecured Creditor.

The statutory order that decides who gets paid first — and who gets whatever's left.

RankWho gets paidOutcome
1Secured creditorsBanks and lenders with collateral — the gym's equipment, leases, assets
2Liquidator fees and costsInsolvency practitioners get paid for the wind-up itself
3Unpaid wagesStatutory preference for staff owed salaries before the close
4Unsecured creditors — prepaid membersGet whatever remains. In practice: near zero.

The $79 member who paid on Sept 9 did not lose a purchase. She made an unsecured loan to a company that stopped trading a day later — and her recovery ranks behind every secured lender, every liquidator invoice and every unpaid wage bill.
