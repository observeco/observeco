First in the Material Is Not First in the Mind

"Singapore's first to use coffee leather" sounds like the strongest possible position. Test it the way you test any claim a customer has to carry.

A supply-chain fact

"First in Singapore to use coffee leather."

Describes what the brand bought — a fabric found at a public trade show. Anyone with a business card and a minimum order can buy it tomorrow.

RecallYes — a clean, memorable line.
FeelNo — describes the input, not the experience.
RepeatNo — a fact, not a word. Try it at dinner.
DefensibleNo — the material is not hers. It is on sale.
vs
A position

"The bag you have to feel."

Built from her own words — "we have lost our connection with craftsmanship and material, how fabric feels on our skin." Names a real problem and picks a fight with online shopping.

RecallYes — one idea, plainly said.
FeelYes — the customer's hand, not the factory.
RepeatYes — repeatable by someone who is not the founder.
DefensibleYes — it makes the hand pleat the proof.
