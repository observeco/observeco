Market curve — how technology usually spreads
Thousands of buyers, each deciding alone

Every buyer must be convinced separately. Slow, uneven, S-shaped. This is where most construction-robot pilots die: the pilot works, the contractor likes it, and nothing else happens — because liking something is not the same as being required to buy it.

Procurement curve — what Singapore did instead
One buyer, already decided, with 800ha behind it

Adoption stops depending on persuasion and starts depending on eligibility. A contractor who wants the pipeline does not get an opinion about autonomous equipment — they get to choose which compliant system to buy.
