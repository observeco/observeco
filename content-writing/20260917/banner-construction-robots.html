The construction robots aren't arriving because they're better.
They're arriving because the biggest buyer wrote them into the tender.

JTC has mandated robotics in its construction tenders since 2025. HDB has put robots into 16 of the 31 Build-To-Order projects tendered since then. Behind both sits a pipeline of 800 hectares of earthworks and S$53 billion in projected demand. That is not a technology trend — it is a procurement schedule, and it has dates on it.
