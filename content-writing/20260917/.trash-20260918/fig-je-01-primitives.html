The output contract — typed, bounded, one of three shapes

What Jev Actually Returns

You declare the possible answers before you ask. Jev picks among them and attaches a calibrated probability. It cannot produce free text, code, or conversation — architecturally, not by policy.

Noul — their word for boolean

"Is this true?"

One probability from 0 to 1. The smallest decision there is.

Returns: probability
Choice

"Which of these fits?"

One option out of up to 255, plus the full spread of probabilities across all options.

Returns: 1 of up to 255 + spread
Score

"Where does this sit?"

A score across 2 to 10 tiers, plus a confidence value. Calibrated, not guessed.

Returns: tier + confidence

Why it's fast: the bucket brigade vs one pass

Normal LLM
One token at a time
Each token conditioned on the last. The bucket has to arrive before the next one starts.
Jev
One forward pass
The answers were fixed in advance, so every candidate is scored at once. No bucket brigade.
Not allowed to invent

If you asked "cat or dog", the answer cannot be "banana". The set of possible answers is closed. That is the source of the speed, the price, and the type safety.
