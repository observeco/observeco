What "Coffee Leather" Actually Is

Not a proprietary invention — a purchased input with four named components. Read it the way you read any spec: what is it made of, and who else can buy it?

Base

Bamboo fibre

The structural substrate of the material.

Foam layer

Sorona polymer

DuPont bio-based PTT — 37% renewable plant content. USDA BioPreferred.

Binder

Water-based resin

Bonds the layers — not a formaldehyde cross-linker.

The signature

Spent coffee grounds

Give it the mellow black hue and the faint coffee smell.
