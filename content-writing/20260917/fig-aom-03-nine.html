The Nine Ways to Differentiate, Scored

There are nine recognised ways to build differentiation. Aomorie scores strongly on the two hardest to say out loud, and leads with the one anyone can buy.

1

Be first — material only

True as a supply fact. The fabric was bought, not invented. Not first in the mind.

2

Own an attribute: touch

The open word. Her own quote names it. Nobody in this corner owns it.

3

Leadership — not available

No scale, no share, one-woman operation.

4

Heritage — not available

Two years old. Heritage cannot be manufactured.

5

Market speciality: the pleat

Expert in one object — the pleated bag — is itself a credential.

6

Next generation — weak

"New material" is a feature claim, matched once the fabric is public.

7

Most preferred — not yet

1,455 followers. No high-energy group claimed.

8

Hot-selling — earned, unused

Boutiques is selective and accepted her. Real social proof, unspent.

9

Production method — the moat

Hand pleating, hours per bag, 10–15 units a batch. Machines cannot copy it.

Undifferentiated — as a brand

No word in the customer's mind. Leading with a material competitors can also buy, and with sustainability — now an entry ticket, not an edge.

Genuinely differentiated — as a maker

The hand pleat, the material in the hand, the founder's taste. All three are real and uncopyable. None of them is in the headline.
