The so-what — three doors for a small Singapore business

The Three Doors the Falling Premium Opens

The falling premium is not only a reason to buy. It is a reason a small operator can build a business around the thing the big incumbents overprice and underdeliver.

1

The SME cyber-hygiene shop Service / product

Meet the three minimums for the thousands of small firms that now want cover: MFA setup, offline back-ups, patch management, done as a service for a nameable fee. The insured firms need exactly this, and most haven't done it yet.

2

The broker/advisor for the under-served firm Distribution / advice

Eight insurers report demand but not distribution depth. A named, vouchable intermediary who translates a small firm's actual risk into the right policy — a word nobody in the category owns yet.

3

The incident-response specialist Recovery / niche

Small firms will be breached; most can't afford a big-firm forensics bill. A niche responder who knows how small shops run — recovery, not the enterprise playbook.*

*These are positioning leads, not settled findings — verify demand and competition before entering. The pattern is the same one that maps everywhere: a tailwind lands hardest in the fragmented, under-served corner — the exact ground a positioned small business wins.
