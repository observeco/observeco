The honest trade — the two ways to pay for a breach

Reject the Premium, Keep the Risk

Insurance is not a cost. It is a choice about when a ransomware bill hits — before it happens (premium) or after (the breach itself).

The uninsured breach The quiet bill
  • Forensic investigators, $200–$500/hour, run for weeks
  • Legal counsel to manage the regulator and the contracts
  • Data restoration and hardware replacement
  • Business outage — revenue lost while locked out of your own systems
  • PDPL fines: up to 10% of annual turnover or S$1M per breach
The insured breach The priced risk
  • S$1,000–$6,000 a year for a small or mid-market firm
  • Insurer's panel of forensic experts + legal, pre-vetted
  • Coverage for recovery, extortion, cloud outages, third-party failures
  • Reputational-recovery expenses now included
  • Breach costs capped at the policy limit, not open-ended
Source: ST (14 Sep 2026), AWG/AXA XL/Howden; PDPL 10% turnover penalty (Oct 2022). Confidence: High. High Confidence
