The price of entry — what a small firm has to show

The Minimums Are Not a Barrier. They Are the Checklist.

Insurers no longer demand a perfect security posture to sell you coverage. They ask three things — and they are things a small business can actually do.

MFA

Multi-factor authentication

Every remote-access portal requires a second step — a code, an authenticator app. The oldest, cheapest defence against the password that leaked.

Back-ups

Offline / disconnected copies

A recovery copy the attacker cannot encrypt, so a ransomware demand is optional, not existential. Backing up costs nothing vs the ask.

Patching

Formal patch management

A routine that fixes high- and critical-severity flaws fast. Slow on the updates is the single clearest signal of an exposed shop.

The shift: a decade of claims data let insurers price risk instead of punishing anyone who was not perfect. The three minimums are achievable hygiene — and the firm that meets them is now insurable, and priced affordably.
